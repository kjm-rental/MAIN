# Motorcycle image sources and approval register

Last reviewed: 2026-10-09 (Asia/Manila)

## Current publication decision

No local motorcycle photograph is currently approved for publication. All ten models use the existing premium placeholder until KJM or an authorized dealer/manufacturer supplies a clean, exact-generation product image with written commercial website permission.

The seven previously published Creative Commons photographs were legally reusable, but they were withdrawn because their streets, parking areas, shops, people, or event backgrounds do not meet KJM's professional dealership-style requirement. The source files are no longer present under `public/images/motorcycles` and are no longer mapped by the catalog.

## Manufacturer licensing audit

- [Yamaha Motor terms](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/copyright/) expressly prohibit commercial or profit-making use of website text, photographs, and illustrations. Yamaha product-page images were not copied.
- [Honda Global terms](https://global.honda/en/terms-and-conditions/) permit only personal, non-commercial internal use unless Honda gives prior express permission. Honda product-page and newsroom images were not copied.
- Suzuki's public product pages and media-bank link did not provide KJM with an express commercial website license during this review. Suzuki product photography was not copied.
- Authorized dealership photography requires written permission identifying the asset and permitted commercial website use; dealership publication alone is not permission.

## Asset acceptance checklist

1. Establish the exact physical-unit year, generation, and variant.
2. Prefer KJM's own full-motorcycle photograph on a white, light gray, or transparent background.
3. For manufacturer or dealer media, retain written commercial website permission naming the asset.
4. Confirm that the permission permits local download, optimization, publication, and any required attribution.
5. Reject watermarks, people, streets, parking areas, busy shops, event backgrounds, incorrect generations, and look-alike models.
6. Prepare a maximum 1,600 px WebP or AVIF derivative while retaining the original aspect ratio.
7. Use descriptive alt text and `object-contain` with consistent padding.
8. Record source, permission, attribution, and verification in `motorcycle_media` before publication.

No production Supabase media record or Storage object was changed during this work.
